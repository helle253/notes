Meh, but petal guard is like a shittier resistance

### Vampire Spawn

### Dream Elemental

S1 seems pretty darn good...

- S1: Projects an aura that inflicts Saving Throw Penalty on all enemies. Enemies that fail the save are affected for the duration of the engagement. Enemies that succeed on the save make the check again every 7 sec.
- S2: When an ally receives a mental status ailment, reacts to immediately remove it and heal that ally for 1~8(1d8+CHA) Heal.

## T5

### Basilisk

Great Innate, S2 seems replacable

Base Attack: 3d6+WIS Nature Damage Spews poisonous gas.

Innate: This unit's Nature Damage ignores the target's Resistance and Immunity. Gains Advantage on attack rolls against enemies with Poisoned.

- M: 3d8+WIS Nature Damage - Inflicts Poisoned\*1 on the target and one enemy behind them.
- S1: Whenever dealing Nature Damage to an enemy, additionally inflicts Weakness\*2.
- S2: When targeted by a melee attack, reacts to inflict Disadvantage on the attacker's attack roll, and has a chance to inflict Blinded\*3.

\*1 While the effect persists, suffers Disadvantage on Attack Roll. Takes 1d4 Nature Damage every 2 sec. (Duration: 6 sec for Basilisk's Venom(B) / 4.8 sec for Breath of Decay (M). Individual Stacks)
\*2 All Attacks and All Saves are reduced by 2
\#3 Vision is obscured. Has Disadvantage on Attack Roll, and attackers gain Advantage on Attack Roll. (Duration: 6 sec)

### Fire Elemental

Solid choice. Innate is superior to Phoenix's, funnily enough. Parts are not that great, so swap them tho.

Base Attack: 3d6+INT Fire Damage (Spell, Ranged)
Innate: Gains 1 Heat each time a fire spell is cast.
M: 2d12+INT Fire Damage (Spell, Melee)
S1: Projects an aura that inflicts Fire Vulnerability on all enemies. Enemies that fail the save are affected for the duration of the engagement. Enemies that succeed on the save make the check again every 7 sec.
S2: When targeted by a melee attack, reacts to reduce incoming damage by 4~32 (4d8) and deal 1~10 (1d10+INT) Bonus Fire Damage to the attacker.

Heat: Fire Damage dealt increases by the number of stacks (Duration: 12 sec, Max Stacks: 10)

### Water Elemental

Decent healer? Might be useful support for Pegasus/Fenrir when kitted out?

### Lich

S1 seems goated for necrotic rooms

- Base Attack: Inflicts Heal Block
- Innate: Get 1 Soul periodically
- M: 3d10+WIS Necrotic Damage (Ranged, Spell, random target) - On hit, has a chance to steal the target's Soul.
- S1: Projects an auro that inflicts Necrotic Vulnerability on enemies all enemies. Enemies that fail the save are affected for the duration of the engagement. Enemies that succeed on the save make the check again every 7 sec.
- S2: When taking damage, reacts to consume 1 Soul and heal self for (3d8+WIS) Heal.

Soul: Necrotic Damage dealt increases by the number of stacks. (Max Stacks: 10)
