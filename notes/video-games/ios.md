# video-games.ios

## Japanese-made (closest vibe)

- Buriedbornes (ohNussy) — Grim, class-based dungeon roguelike with tons of passives/affixes and very fast runs. Strong auto/assist options and deep buildcraft. F2P with optional unlocks.
- Anomaly Collapse (PC, premium) — Anime‑style grid tactics roguelite with relic/party synergies.
- B100X: Auto Dungeon RPG (ohNussy) — Plan your build and AI, then your character auto-delves while you iterate gear and passives between runs. Spiky, numbers-heavy JP feel. F2P with IAP.
- Rogue with the Dead (room6/keizo) — Road‑push auto battler: draft artifacts/skills, tune comps, and snowball a run. Great JP indie presentation; bite-sized, theorycrafty.
- Labyrinth of the Witch (Orange Cube) — Compact Shiren-like with cute JP art; tight item/positioning puzzles, generous QoL for mobile. Premium base, some DLC.
- DragonFang (Toydea) — Mystery‑Dungeon lineage with the “Fang” equipment system that changes your kit. Availability skews JP App Store; global access can vary.
- Yodanji — ASCII-style roguelike themed entirely around yokai. Not an autobattler, but very JP in tone and systems; quick, crunchy runs.
- Soul Knight / Soul Knight Prequel (iOS/Android, F2P/premium‑leaning) — Fast roguelite action; tons of gear/class synergies.

## Anime-JP vibe (not JP dev, but nails the style)

- Arknights (Hypergryph/Yostar) — Tower defense with an exceptional pure roguelite mode (Integrated Strategies). You draft relics, routes, and operators; feels like a JP roguelike grafted onto a TD great. F2P, generous.
- Honkai: Star Rail (HoYoverse) — Its Simulated Universe mode is a true roguelite: pick Blessings/Curios, pivot paths, and abuse synergies. Auto-battle option exists; anime presentation is top tier. F2P.

## JP-flavored “set it up, watch it go” (adjacent, comfy)

- Dungeon Village 1 and 2 (Kairosoft) — Build a JRPG adventurer town; heroes auto-fight while you stack facility synergies and buffs. Premium, offline, classic Kairosoft vibe.
- Beastie Bay (Kairosoft) — Monster-collection survival/base-builder with cozy JP pixel art and lots of passive stacking. Premium/DX versions available.
- Kingdom Adventurers (Kairosoft) — Recruit parties, clear dungeons semi-automatically, and kit them via town upgrades. Light roguelite feel across expeditions.
- The Battle Cats (PONOS) — Iconic JP lane defense with lineup “combos” and unit synergies; not roguelite, but scratches the same comp-building, watch-it-pop itch. F2P.

## Anime roguelite deckbuilders/auto-battlers with strong synergy

- Limbus Company — Mirror Dungeon is a true roguelite run with identity/passive synergies; stylish anime look.
- Library of Ruina — Complex party deckbattler with page/passive builds; very anime presentation.
- Phantom Rose 2 Sapphire (PC) / Phantom Rose Scarlet (mobile) — Pure anime deckbuilder roguelites; clean, fast runs.

- Alina of the Arena — Hex tactics + deckbuilding; compact runs, satisfying scaling.
- One Step From Eden — Rapid-fire spell deckbuilder with anime-pixel flair and wild combo potential.
